Team chat
=========

**Team chat** is the real-time messenger for the people you work with in BizKitHub. You write one-to-one messages, talk in groups and department channels, send files and voice messages, run polls, and start audio or video calls without leaving the administration. Customers never see team chat; for chatbot and customer threads, see [Conversations](/conversations-overview).

Team chat belongs to you as a person, not to one organisation. If you are a member of several organisations, all your conversations are in one inbox, and a conversation stays the same whichever organisation you are signed in to.

## When you would use it

- Ask a colleague a quick question instead of sending an e-mail.
- Coordinate a team in a group, for example *Christmas campaign*, with shared files and pinned decisions.
- Keep a whole department informed through a channel whose members follow the organisation structure automatically.
- Turn a request from a message into a task with one click.
- Call a colleague, share your screen and agree on the next steps.

## Where to find it in the admin

Open **Communication → Chat** for the full-page view. Conversations also open as floating windows at the bottom of any admin page: up to six windows, of which one to three are visible side by side depending on the screen width. The keyboard shortcut `Ctrl+Shift+M` (`⌘+Shift+M` on a Mac) opens **New message**.

## Who you can write to

You can start a conversation with anyone who is an active member of at least one organisation you also belong to. An organisation can turn off **Team chat introductions** in its settings; its members then no longer find each other through that organisation, but existing conversations continue. A one-to-one conversation you already have always stays available, even when you no longer share an organisation.

## Conversation types

| Type | Shown as | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Notes to self | **Notes to self** | A private space for drafts and notes. Reminders are delivered here. |
| One-to-one | The colleague's name | Exactly one conversation per pair of people. |
| Group | The group name | 2 to 50 participants including you. The creator becomes the **Owner**. |
| Department channel | The department name | A group for everyone in a department and its sub-departments. |

### Groups

Create a group with **New group**, or select several people in **New conversation** and click **Create group**. The name can be 1 to 100 characters long.

Every participant can **Rename** the group and **Add people**. A person who left and is added again gets the earlier history back. Only the owner can **Remove from group**. Anyone can **Leave group**; when the owner leaves, the longest-standing participant becomes the owner.

### Department channels

A member who manages the organisation structure can turn on **Team chat channel** for a department. The channel then contains every active member of the department and its sub-departments, and its name follows the department name. Nobody can add, remove or rename by hand; the channel updates itself when the structure changes or when a member is blocked. Turning the channel off hides it; turning it on again brings the history back.

## Writing messages

Press `Enter` to send and `Shift+Enter` for a new line. A message can be up to 10,000 characters long and supports this formatting:

```text
**bold**   *italics*   _italics_   ~~strikethrough~~   `code`   @mention
```

Links are clickable, and a link to an order or a task in the administration is shown as a chip with its name. For the first web link in a message, the chat shows a preview with the page title and image.

Unsent text is saved as a **Draft** for each conversation and is available on your other devices.

### Mentions

Type `@` and pick a colleague. Only participants of the conversation can be mentioned. A mention appears in the colleague's **Activity** tab and reaches them even when they muted the conversation.

### Files and voice messages

Add files with **Attach files**, by pasting, or by dropping them onto the conversation. The limits are:

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Files per message | 10 |
| Size of one file | 100 MB |
| Upload per person per day | 2 GB |
| Voice message length | 300 seconds |

Chat files are private to the conversation and never appear in [Drive](/drive-overview). **Shared files** in the conversation details lists the latest 200 files.

Use **Record a voice message** when the message box is empty. The recipient can play it at 1×, 1.5× or 2× speed.

### Replies and threads

- **Reply** quotes the original message in the main timeline.
- **Reply in thread** opens a side **Thread** panel. Thread replies do not appear in the main timeline; the original message shows the number of replies and the time of the last one.

### Editing, deleting and reactions

You can edit and delete only your own messages, with no time limit. Press the up arrow in an empty message box to edit your last message. An edited message shows *edited*. A deleted message shows *This message was deleted* to the others; its text, files and reactions are removed.

Use **Add reaction** to react with any emoji, up to 10 different emoji per message. Double-click a message to add or remove 👍.

### Pinning, forwarding and saving

- **Pin** keeps up to 20 messages per conversation in a bar at the top. Any participant can pin.
- **Forward** sends a copy, including its files, to another conversation or person. The copy is marked *Forwarded* and does not name the original author.
- **Save message** adds the message to your private **Saved** list (up to 1,000 messages).

## Reminders and scheduled messages

**Remind me** on a message offers *In 20 minutes*, *In an hour*, *In 3 hours*, *Tomorrow at 9:00*, *Next Monday at 9:00* or a custom time up to one year ahead. The reminder arrives in **Notes to self** with a **Go to message** link. You can have up to 50 pending reminders.

**Send later** schedules a text message for a time between one minute and 90 days ahead. Scheduled messages cannot contain files. A bar above the message box lists them with **Send now** and **Cancel**. If a scheduled message cannot be delivered, for example because you left the group, it is marked *Not sent* and is not retried.

## Polls

Click **Poll**, enter a **Question** (up to 300 characters) and 2 to 10 **Options** (up to 120 characters each). Choose whether to **Allow multiple answers** and whether to use **Anonymous voting**, in which voter names are hidden from everyone. Participants can change or withdraw their vote. The author can **Close poll**; the results stay visible.

## Calls and meetings

**Call** and **Video call** in the conversation header start a call with everyone in the conversation. During the call you can mute, turn the camera on or off, **Share screen** with sound and choose your **Devices**. A call rings for 45 seconds, ends when nobody has been connected for 60 seconds, and lasts at most 12 hours. Calls are not recorded. A group call continues while at least one person is connected, and late joiners see **Join**.

**Schedule a meeting** plans a call in the conversation, with a title, start, duration (5 minutes to 8 hours) and optional description. Every participant gets a meeting card with **Add to calendar (.ics)** and a reminder 5 minutes before the start. **Join** on the card starts the call. The **Meetings in chat** panel lists meetings for the next 14 days across all your organisations.

## AI assistance

- **What did I miss?** summarises your unread messages, or the latest 60 when you have read everything, in three to six points, and lists questions and mentions that wait for you. When you open a conversation with 8 or more unread messages, the chat offers the summary.
- **Translate** translates a message into your language and keeps emoji, links and code.
- **Suggest a reply** proposes up to three short replies to the latest messages.

The AI assistance runs only when you ask for it. The conversation content is not stored by these features.

## Creating a task from a message

Choose **Create task** in the message menu. The **Task title** is pre-filled from the message; optionally tick **Assign to me** and click **Create task**. The task is created in the organisation you are signed in to, with the message quoted and a link back to the chat. In the other direction, **Send to team chat** on an order or a task detail shares it into a conversation, with an optional note. See [Issues](/issue-overview).

## Conversation settings

The conversation details hold settings that apply to you only: **Mute**, **Pin** (pinned conversations are listed first) and **Archive** (hidden until a new message arrives). **Mark as unread** and **Mark all as read** manage your read state. The **Conversation theme** colour is shared with all participants.

In a one-to-one conversation you can **Block** the other person. Neither of you can then write in that conversation, start a call, or add the other to a new group. Groups you already share are not affected.

## Search

The search box finds people, conversations and messages. Message search needs at least 3 characters, ignores accents, covers only conversations you are a participant in, and returns the 30 newest matches. Nobody, including root members of an organisation, can search conversations they are not part of.
