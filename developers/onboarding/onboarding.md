Onboarding
==========

Complete guide to registration and account creation in the BizKitHub system. We ensure a smooth entry into the BizKitHub ecosystem with a clear understanding of features, capabilities, and next steps for effective platform utilization.

## Why Onboard

- **Quick start** — registration takes only a few minutes.
- **Security** — your data is secure.
- **Instant access** — right after email verification.
- **24/7 support** — we are here for you anytime.

## Main Registration Steps

Registration is quick and simple. Just follow these three steps.

### 1. User Account Registration

Create a personal account for the individual who will log into the system.

- Fill in basic personal information
- Verify email address
- Set up a secure password
- Activate account

### 2. Create or Join Organization

Create a new organization or gain access to an existing organization.

- Choose between new or existing organization
- Fill in organization details (if new)
- Assign roles and permissions
- Set up basic configuration

### 3. Welcome Onboarding Email

Automatic welcome email sent with important information.

- Registration confirmation
- Documentation links
- Support contacts
- Next steps and recommendations

## Choose Your Path

After completing basic registration, the process diverges depending on whether you are creating a new organization or joining an existing one.

### New Organization (Full Control)

Become an administrator of a new organization with full rights to configure and manage it.

1. Fill in organization details
2. Basic system setup
3. Configure access permissions
4. Administrator onboarding email
5. Access to documentation and support

You will receive an **Administrator Onboarding Email**, sent automatically upon completion.

### Existing Organization (Quick Join)

Join as a member of an existing organization with predefined permissions.

1. Confirm organization membership
2. Assign roles and permissions
3. Review organization guidelines
4. Welcome email with instructions
5. Contact organization administrator

You will receive a **Member Welcome Email**, sent automatically upon completion.

## Onboarding Email Content

After successful registration, you will receive an email with important information based on your role.

### Organization Administrator

- BizKitHub system fundamentals
- Organization management guide
- Technical support contacts
- Documentation links
- Tips for effective platform use

### Organization Member

- Organization-specific instructions
- Organization administrator contact
- Basic usage guide
- Organization rules and procedures
- Relevant documentation links

## Tips for Successful Onboarding

- Use a valid email address
- Fill in all required fields correctly
- Read the onboarding email carefully
- Contact support if you encounter issues
