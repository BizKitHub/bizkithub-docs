## Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes above. Typical periods: account data — for the lifetime of the account plus 3 years; transactional data — 10 years (statutory accounting retention); technical logs — 12 months. Actual retention per data class is documented in the Records of Processing Activities.

## Your rights

As a data subject, you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate data.
- Request erasure ("right to be forgotten"), subject to statutory limits.
- Restrict or object to processing.
- Receive your data in a portable format.
- Withdraw consent at any time (without affecting past lawful processing).
- Lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection ([uoou.cz](https://www.uoou.cz)).
