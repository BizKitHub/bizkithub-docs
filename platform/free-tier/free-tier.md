Free plan
=========

The **Free** plan lets you run a real business on BizKitHub at no cost and with no time limit. It is meant for getting started and for small projects; when you need more, you move to a paid plan without migrating any data.

## What the Free plan includes

- **Bookings and calendar.** Up to 100 bookings a month with the basic calendar.
- **E-mail notifications** to you and to your customers.
- **Mobile access** to the administration.
- **Basic support** through [support.bizkithub.com](https://support.bizkithub.com).

You create the plan without a credit card, and it never expires.

## Changing plans

You can move to a higher or lower plan at any time. The change applies immediately, and a paid plan is billed only for the prorated period. All data stays where it is: a higher plan unlocks features on the same organisation.

If you cancel a paid plan, the organisation keeps its data for 30 days, so you can change your mind or export what you need.
