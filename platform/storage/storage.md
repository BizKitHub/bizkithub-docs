Storage
=======

BizKitHub stores two kinds of data: **structured records** (contacts, orders, invoices, settings and everything else with fields) and **files** (images, PDFs, attachments, imports). This article explains where each kind lives, how files are delivered and how access to them is controlled.

## Structured records

Records live in a relational database in the European Union. Every record belongs to exactly one organisation and every query the platform runs is limited to the organisation of the current user or API key. Records are covered by the [backup and recovery](/backup-overview) process.

## Files

Files are kept in S3-compatible object storage, separately from the database. The storage keeps redundant copies of every file, and a file counts as uploaded only after it has been stored durably.

Files reach the storage in several ways:

- **Uploads in the administration**, for example product images, post galleries, contact avatars and attachments of tickets or e-mails.
- **Files generated by the platform**, such as invoice and order PDFs, exports and shipping labels.
- **Files received from outside**, for example attachments of incoming e-mails or documents sent to the document inbox.

## Delivery

Public files, such as product images and images in posts, are delivered through a global content delivery network from the location closest to the visitor.
Private files, such as invoices, contracts or ticket attachments, are not listed anywhere public. They are reachable from the administration, or through the unique link the platform sends to the person who should see the file, for example the invoice link in an order e-mail.

## Encryption

All traffic to and from storage uses TLS. Files in the object storage are encrypted at rest by the storage provider.

## Deleting files

Deleting a file in the administration removes it from the organisation immediately. Copies may remain in backups until those backups expire. When an organisation is deleted, its files are removed together with its records.
